About Us

Downrange Robotics was founded on a simple idea: training should look like the real thing. Static paper targets can't replicate movement, decision-making, or stress — so we build machines that can.

Designed and built in Arizona, our systems combine rugged hardware with intelligent software to deliver training experiences that were previously only available to elite units.
